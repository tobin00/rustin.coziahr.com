Why “destinations”?

I use “destinations” because this map includes countries plus territories and other separately tracked places, such as Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, and Puerto Rico.

The country count uses the 193 United Nations member states as its baseline. The remaining entries are labeled “Other destination” in the lists.

Each destination code counts once. England and Scotland share the United Kingdom’s code, GB. U.S. states stay in their own breakdown, and upcoming trips add no visits.

Each destination belongs to one continent for this list. North America includes Central America and the Caribbean.
